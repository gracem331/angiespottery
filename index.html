About Angie

A weekly ritual, over a decade in the making.

By day, Angie has spent 20+ years working in corporate Austin — the kind of career that fills a calendar and empties a creative tank. So about ten years ago, she carved out something just for herself: a weekly pottery class that never stopped.

Every week, without fail, she shows up at the wheel. Not to sell, not to build a brand — just to make something with her hands. Over the years, those pieces have become gifts for friends, housewarming presents, birthday surprises, and a growing collection that now fills more shelves than her home can hold.

That's where you come in. The pieces available here are the extras — the ones that deserve a home and a purpose. Some are for sale, some might even be free to the right person. And if you have something specific in mind, she's open to custom requests too (just know those take time — real, unhurried, kiln-fired time).

Everything here was made in Austin, TX, by someone who genuinely loves making it.

Custom requests

Have something specific in mind?

Angie is open to custom requests — a specific mug size, a glaze color you love, a set of bowls for your kitchen. She'll let you know if it's something she can take on.

Custom pieces work around her weekly studio schedule and the kiln's firing calendar. If you're in a hurry, this probably isn't the right fit. If you're okay waiting for something made with real care — send a note.

⏳ Heads up: custom pieces can take anywhere from a few weeks to several months depending on complexity and the firing schedule. Angie will always be upfront about timing before committing.
